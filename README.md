# SWYNEX Task 2 - Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## Objective

The objective of this task is to perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a cleaned online shopping sales dataset using Microsoft Excel. The analysis focuses on understanding sales patterns, customer orders, payment methods, products, categories, cities, and monthly sales trends.

## Dataset

The dataset contains online shopping sales records with information such as order date, customer name, city, product, category, quantity, unit price, payment method, and total spending.

## EDA Process

The following analysis was performed using Microsoft Excel:

- Summary statistics
- City-wise sales analysis
- Category-wise sales analysis
- Payment method analysis
- Product-wise sales analysis
- Monthly sales trend analysis
- Data visualization using charts
- Identification of key business insights

## Key Findings

- Mumbai recorded the highest total sales among the cities, with sales of ₹198,200.
- Electronics generated the highest category sales, with total sales of ₹383,400.
- January recorded the highest monthly sales, with total sales of ₹359,500.
- Card was the most frequently used payment method, with 19 orders.
- Mixer generated the highest sales among the individual products, with total sales of ₹54,400.

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- GitHub

## Conclusion

The EDA helped identify important sales patterns and trends in the dataset. The analysis and visualizations provide a clear understanding of sales performance across cities, categories, products, payment methods, and months.
